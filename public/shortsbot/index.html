shortsbot

A personal video tool by Novaro.

shortsbot automatically makes short, music-synced "top list" videos (for example, "The 7 wonders of the world") from licensed stock footage and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It is run privately by the channel owner on their own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.
  • It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • Background footage comes from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License.
