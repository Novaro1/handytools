shortsbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

shortsbot is a personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to make short videos and upload them to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

shortsbot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

What data it accesses

shortsbot asks for one Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in channel. It does not read, collect or store any other information from your Google account, such as your email, contacts, watch history, comments or analytics.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only in a file on the owner's own computer, readable by the owner alone. It is never sent anywhere except Google's servers, to upload videos. shortsbot has no server and no database, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Sharing

shortsbot does not sell, share or transfer any user data to anyone.

Revoking access

You can remove shortsbot's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes it.

Contact

Questions: reach the owner through github.com/Novaro1.
